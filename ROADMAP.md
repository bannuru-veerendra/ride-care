# RideCare Product Roadmap

## Planned Features

1. Fuel and mileage tracking  
   - Track kilometers traveled, fuel volume, and price per liter.

2. Service history and reminders  
   - Record previous services and schedule upcoming maintenance.  
   - Capture service details such as general service, chain sprocket replacement, brake pad replacement, and more.

3. Insurance management  
   - Store insurance documents (PDF).  
   - Track policy details and expiration dates.

4. Document vault  
   - Store key bike documents such as Driving License (DL) and Registration Certificate (RC) PDFs.

5. Maintenance guidance  
   - Provide practical maintenance recommendations (for example, engine oil change intervals and chain lubrication timing).

## Future Scope

- AI-powered enhancements, such as live fuel price insights and personalized responses to rider questions.
